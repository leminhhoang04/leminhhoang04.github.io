Minh-Hoang Le

I am a senior undergraduate student at University of Science - VNUHCM, majoring in the Advanced Program in Computer Science. I am currently an undergraduate student member at SELAB, where I work on deep learning and computer vision under the guidance of Assoc. Prof. Minh-Triet Tran and MSc. Trong-Le Do. My recent work primarily revolves around visual forensics and multimedia retrieval. Before diving into AI research, I spent years in competitive programming and proudly represented Vietnam at the ICPC World Finals in 2022 and 2024.

Email  /  CV  /  Scholar  /  Github

Research

I am interested in computer vision, deep learning, and image forensics. Most of my research is about developing robust detection systems to identify AI-generated manipulations, localized image forgeries, and deepfakes under extreme conditions and cross-domain settings. I also work on multimedia retrieval, from clip-based video event search to event-aware image captioning with vision-language models.
